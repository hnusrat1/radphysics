These are estimates from a crude in-house Monte Carlo, not validated transport-code output. No transport code or tabulated XCOM/ESTAR data were available offline. The main caveats:

- **Photon spectra:** the 2 MeV primaries lie outside the 0–1.4 MeV photon grid, so the primary spectrum is zero in every bin. Scattered photons above 1.4 MeV are also not binned, so the scattered spectrum does not sum to the total scattered fluence.
- **Cross sections:** Compton uses Klein-Nishina (0.0489 cm²/g at 2 MeV). Photoelectric, pair and coherent terms are rough approximations contributing about 1–2% of interactions.
- **Electrons:** the Layer 3–4 electron quantities come from an infinite-medium (CPE) recursion with Møller delta rays. Bremsstrahlung and fluorescence are neglected, and the electron angular distribution is approximated from the source distribution.
- **Depth dose:** the PDD5 value comes from a straight-ahead electron deposition model with no lateral transport, so it is the least reliable number here.
- **Dose:** D is taken as K_col under CPE, so β = 1.00 by construction.
