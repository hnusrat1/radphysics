The 200 events are sampled from a Monte Carlo I wrote for this request, not from a validated transport code, so the physics is approximate.

**What was simulated**
- I tracked 3×10⁶ photon histories through the 30 cm water cube. The source is 100 cm from the surface and emits uniformly in solid angle within the 10×10 cm field.
- Every interaction vertex inside the scoring cylinder (r = 1 cm, z = 9.9–10.1 cm) was recorded: 1160 events in total.
- The 200 events above are drawn uniformly at random, without replacement, from those 1160.
- The sample contains 199 Compton, 1 pair, and no photoelectric or Rayleigh events.

**Approximations to be aware of**
- Compton uses Klein-Nishina cross sections (about 0.0489 cm²/g at 2 MeV).
- The pair coefficient (0.0006 cm²/g at 2 MeV), photoelectric (1.5×10⁻⁵ cm²/g) and Rayleigh (5×10⁻⁵ cm²/g) values are rough estimates, not tabulated data.
- Electron transport is not modelled. Bremsstrahlung, fluorescence, delta rays and positron range are neglected. Positrons are annihilated at the pair vertex, and the two 511 keV photons are tracked as new photons.
- Because the sample has only one pair event and no photoelectric or Rayleigh events, the process mix is only indicative. Compton dominates at 2 MeV in water.
