Here is the configuration, with the values and the caveats that matter most.

**What was computed**

- **Primary photons** are analytic: exp(−μ·5 cm) for each line, averaged over the 0.2 cm scoring slab, with the inverse-square factor cancelling against Φ₀.
- **Scattered photons** come from a photon Monte Carlo of 2×10⁷ histories. It uses the Klein–Nishina Compton kinematics, XCOM coefficients and photo/pair absorption, with annihilation photons. The scoring is track-length in the 1 cm radius, 0.2 cm thick cylinder at 5 cm.
- **Electrons** (Layers 3–5): photon-generated electrons come from the MC interactions and the analytic primary interactions. Delta rays are added with Møller cross sections. The fluence follows from a continuous slowing-down solution using ESTAR collision stopping powers. The angular distribution uses a Highland multiple-scattering walk.
- **Dose** is taken as K_col (charged-particle equilibrium), so β = 1. g comes from the ESTAR radiation yield.
- **s_w,air** uses ESTAR water with a Bethe-formula air ratio, a 10 keV restricted Spencer–Attix form, and a track-end term.
- **PDD(5 cm)** uses the 0.25 cm depth-binned kerma with a forward electron build-up kernel (λ = 0.1 cm), which puts d_max at ~0.5 cm.

**Main approximations and uncertainties**

- No electron transport was simulated. Layers 3–5 are analytic or model-based, so expect roughly 5–10% uncertainty there, and larger for the angular and delta-fraction values.
- Rayleigh scattering uses a Thomson-like angular distribution without form factors. Its effect on the scattered fluence is small.
- No air stopping-power table was provided, so the air term relies on a Bethe-formula ratio.
- Photoelectric electrons are taken at full energy, and Compton kinematics ignore electron binding.
- The scattered-fluence statistical error is about 1%.
