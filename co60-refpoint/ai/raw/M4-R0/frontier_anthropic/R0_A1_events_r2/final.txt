**What was done**
- Primary interactions in the scoring cylinder were scored analytically from the uncollided fluence, with exponential attenuation to the 5 cm depth.
- Scattered photons came from an analog transport of every collision in the water cube, with next-event estimation toward the cylinder.
- Compton used Klein-Nishina; Rayleigh used a simple form-factor model; pair production emitted two 511 keV annihilation photons that were transported in turn.
- Photoelectric and pair cross sections, and the Rayleigh form factor, are rough parameterisations. Electron transport, bremsstrahlung and binding effects are omitted.
- Consistency check: a track-length estimate of the scattered contribution agreed with the next-event estimate.
- The 200 events were drawn by systematic sampling proportional to weight, without replacement.
