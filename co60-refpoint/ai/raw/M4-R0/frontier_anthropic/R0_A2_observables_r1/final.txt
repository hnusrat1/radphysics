## Summary

These are estimates from a custom photon Monte Carlo plus analytic electron models. No radiation transport code was available, so I wrote the photon transport and next-event scoring myself. Electron transport is a continuous-slowing-down model with Møller knock-ons, not a full electron Monte Carlo. Treat values as approximate, roughly ±5–10% for the photon fluence quantities and more for the electron-side quantities.

**Known gaps:**
- The L4 angular distribution is the initial-direction distribution. I wrote a range-weighted version but ran out of tool calls before executing it.
- Photoelectric and pair electron directions are taken as the photon direction.
- Spencer–Attix for air uses a Bethe scaling from water (air data were not in the files).
- The PDD uses an electron build-up convolution, because the pure collision-kerma profile peaks at the surface.
- β = 1 assumes collision-kerma equilibrium at the point.

## Method

1. **Primary fluence.** Analytic: for each line, 0.5·exp(−μ_tot·5 cm)·(105/105)², with XCOM total attenuation including coherent. Its bins are 1.1732 MeV in bin 23 (1.15–1.2) and 1.3325 MeV in bin 26 (1.3–1.35). Primary photons are all in the 0.9–1.0 cosθ bin.
2. **Scattered fluence.** Photon Monte Carlo of 3×10⁶ source photons (three seeds, ~1% seed-to-seed agreement) in the 30 cm water cube. Sampling used the 10×10 pyramid at SSD 100 cm, uniform in solid angle. Interactions used XCOM coefficients, Klein–Nishina Compton with Kahn-style sampling, Rayleigh, and pair production with annihilation photons. Scoring used next-event estimation to a 7-point average over the 1 cm-radius cylinder at each depth. Normalization used Φ₀ = 1/(Ω_p·105²) per emitted photon.
3. **Interaction and energy-transfer fractions.** Computed from fluence × μ_i(E), with KN-averaged energy transfer for Compton.
4. **Electron source spectra (Layer 3).** Klein–Nishina electron spectra and directions for each arrival bin, with pair electrons split uniformly, plus photoelectrons.
5. **Electron fluence (Layer 4).** Steady-state CSDA slowing-down with Møller knock-on production, using ESTAR total stopping power.
6. **Dose (Layer 5).** K = Σ Φ·E·μ_tr, K_col = K·(1−g), g from ESTAR radiation yields, D = K_col (β = 1). Spencer–Attix uses restricted Møller losses with Δ = 10 keV and a track-end term.
7. **PDD.** K_col profile at 0.25–5 cm, convolved with an exponential electron kernel (λ = 0.09 cm) so that d_max ≈ 0.5 cm.

## Data sources

- XCOM-style water coefficients from `/tmp/data/xcom_water.txt` (total with coherent for attenuation).
- ESTAR-style water stopping powers, CSDA range and radiation yields from `/tmp/data/estar_water.txt`.
- Klein–Nishina and Møller cross sections analytic (standard formulas).
- Air stopping-power ratio via Bethe scaling (I_air = 85.7 eV, Z/A = 0.49919) from water ESTAR values.
