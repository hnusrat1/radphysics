We are studying how photons and electrons deposit energy at one point in a water phantom. Report the
quantities below for this configuration.

Configuration:
- Phantom: liquid water, 30 x 30 x 30 cm^3 cube, density 1.000 g/cm^3.
- Outside the phantom: vacuum.
- Source: point source on the central axis, 100 cm from the phantom surface (SSD 100 cm), emitting photons uniformly in solid angle within the pyramid that defines a 10 cm x 10 cm field at the phantom surface; no collimator, capsule or other scatter.
- Photon energies: monoenergetic photons, 2.0 MeV.
- Point of interest: central axis, 10.0 cm depth.
- Scoring volume: cylinder on the beam axis, radius 1.0 cm, thickness 0.2 cm, centred at 10.0 cm depth.
- Normalization: Phi0 is the primary photon fluence at the location of the point of interest, 110 cm from the source, with the phantom removed.
- Depth dose: D(d_max) is the maximum of the central-axis depth dose for this beam.

Definitions:
- Primary photon: a photon that has not interacted since leaving the source. Scattered photon: every other
  photon (scattered source photons and secondary photons such as bremsstrahlung, fluorescence and annihilation
  photons).
- theta: the angle between a particle's direction and the beam axis (the direction from the source towards the
  phantom), so cos(theta) = 1 is straight down the beam axis.
- Fluence: track length per unit volume in the scoring volume. Spectra are fluence per energy bin (not per MeV),
  divided by Phi0.
- Delta rays: electrons set in motion by electrons. They are part of the electron fluence (Layer 4) and not of
  the electrons set in motion by photon interactions (Layer 3).
- Quantities "at the point" refer to the scoring volume.

Bin edges (MeV for energies; each list gives the edges, so a list of n+1 numbers defines n bins):
- photon_energy (28 bins): 0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3, 0.35, 0.4, 0.45, 0.5, 0.55, 0.6, 0.65, 0.7, 0.75, 0.8, 0.85, 0.9, 0.95, 1, 1.05, 1.1, 1.15, 1.2, 1.25, 1.3, 1.35, 1.4
- electron_energy (25 bins; the last bin is wider): 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3, 0.35, 0.4, 0.45, 0.5, 0.55, 0.6, 0.65, 0.7, 0.75, 0.8, 0.85, 0.9, 0.95, 1, 1.05, 1.1, 1.15, 1.2, 1.4
- cos_theta (10 bins): -1, -0.8, -0.6, -0.4, -0.2, 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 1

Quantities to report (JSON key, unit and shape in brackets):
Layer 1. Photons arriving at the point
- L1_primary_fluence_per_phi0 [dimensionless; number]: Primary photon fluence in the scoring volume (photons that have not interacted since leaving the source, both lines), divided by Phi0.
- L1_total_fluence_per_phi0 [dimensionless; number]: Total photon fluence in the scoring volume (primary plus scattered), divided by Phi0.
- L1_scattered_fraction_fluence [dimensionless; number]: Scattered fraction of the photon fluence in the scoring volume.
- L1_scattered_fraction_energy_fluence [dimensionless; number]: Scattered fraction of the photon energy fluence in the scoring volume.
- L1_mean_photon_energy_MeV [MeV; number]: Fluence-weighted mean energy of all photons (primary and scattered) in the scoring volume.
- L1_photon_fluence_spectrum_primary [dimensionless (fluence in the bin divided by Phi0); list of 28 numbers on the photon_energy grid]: Primary photon fluence in each photon energy bin, divided by Phi0.
- L1_photon_fluence_spectrum_scattered [dimensionless (fluence in the bin divided by Phi0); list of 28 numbers on the photon_energy grid]: Scattered photon fluence in each photon energy bin, divided by Phi0.
- L1_angular_primary [fraction; list of 10 numbers on the cos_theta grid, summing to 1]: Fraction of the primary photon fluence in each cos(theta) bin.
- L1_angular_scattered [fraction; list of 10 numbers on the cos_theta grid, summing to 1]: Fraction of the scattered photon fluence in each cos(theta) bin.

Layer 2. Photon interactions at the point
- L2_interaction_fractions [fraction; object with keys compton, photoelectric, pair, rayleigh]: Fraction of all photon interactions in the scoring volume by process: incoherent (Compton) scattering, photoelectric absorption, pair production (nuclear and electron field), coherent (Rayleigh) scattering.
- L2_energy_transfer_fractions [fraction; object with keys compton, photoelectric, pair, rayleigh]: The same fractions weighted by the energy transferred to charged particles.
- L2_interaction_fractions_primary [fraction; object with keys compton, photoelectric, pair, rayleigh]: Fraction of interactions by process, for interactions of primary photons only.
- L2_interaction_fractions_scattered [fraction; object with keys compton, photoelectric, pair, rayleigh]: Fraction of interactions by process, for interactions of scattered photons only.
- L2_scattered_share_of_interactions [dimensionless; number]: Fraction of all photon interactions in the scoring volume that are interactions of scattered photons.

Layer 3. Electrons set in motion
- L3_initial_spectrum [fraction; list of 25 numbers on the electron_energy grid, summing to 1]: Distribution of the initial kinetic energy of electrons and positrons set in motion in the scoring volume by photon interactions (not delta rays): fraction in each electron energy bin, normalized over the bins.
- L3_initial_spectrum_parent_primary [fraction; list of 25 numbers on the electron_energy grid, summing to 1]: As L3_initial_spectrum, for electrons and positrons set in motion by primary photons only.
- L3_mean_initial_energy_MeV [MeV; number]: Mean initial kinetic energy of all electrons and positrons set in motion by photon interactions.
- L3_mean_initial_energy_parent_primary_MeV [MeV; number]: Mean initial kinetic energy of electrons and positrons set in motion by primary photons.
- L3_initial_direction [fraction; list of 10 numbers on the cos_theta grid, summing to 1]: Distribution of the initial direction of those electrons and positrons: fraction in each cos(theta) bin, theta measured from the beam axis.

Layer 4. Electrons slowing down
- L4_electron_fluence_per_phi0 [dimensionless; number]: Electron fluence in the scoring volume above 10 keV, all electrons including delta rays (positrons excluded), divided by Phi0.
- L4_electron_fluence_spectrum [dimensionless (fluence in the bin divided by Phi0); list of 25 numbers on the electron_energy grid]: Electron fluence in each electron energy bin, all electrons including delta rays, divided by Phi0.
- L4_delta_fraction [dimensionless; number]: Fraction of the electron fluence above 10 keV that is carried by delta rays (electrons set in motion by electrons).
- L4_mean_electron_energy_MeV [MeV; number]: Fluence-weighted mean energy of the electron fluence above 10 keV.
- L4_angular [fraction; list of 10 numbers on the cos_theta grid, summing to 1]: Fraction of the electron fluence in each cos(theta) bin, theta measured from the beam axis.
- L4_radiative_to_collision [dimensionless; number]: Ratio of radiative to collision energy loss for the electrons in the scoring volume.

Layer 5. Energy deposited
- L5_D_over_phi0_Gy_cm2 [Gy cm^2; number]: Absorbed dose to water in the scoring volume per unit Phi0.
- L5_K_over_phi0_Gy_cm2 [Gy cm^2; number]: Kerma in water at the point per unit Phi0.
- L5_Kcol_over_phi0_Gy_cm2 [Gy cm^2; number]: Collision kerma in water at the point per unit Phi0.
- L5_beta [dimensionless; number]: beta = D / K_col at the point.
- L5_g [dimensionless; number]: Radiative yield g = 1 - K_col / K.
- L5_dose_fraction_parent_scattered [dimensionless; number]: Fraction of the dose deposited by electrons whose parent photon was a scattered photon.
- L5_s_w_air [dimensionless; number]: Spencer-Attix water-to-air stopping-power ratio at the point, cut-off Delta = 10 keV, including the track-end term. State which stopping-power data you used.
- L5_PDD5_percent [percent; number]: Percent depth dose at 5 cm on the central axis, 100 x D(5 cm) / D(d_max).

Give a value for every quantity. If you are unsure, give your best estimate rather than leaving it out.
Also give "method": a short description of how you obtained the values (at most 150 words), and
"data_sources": the cross-section and stopping-power data you relied on.
