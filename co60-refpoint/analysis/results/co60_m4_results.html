Co-60 reference point · M4 AI runs · scored against Monte Carlo

Five fast models at the Co-60 reference point

R0: central axis, 5 cm depth, water, 10 × 10 cm², SSD 100 cm 124 conversations5 models × 3 conditionsrun 2026-10-12 branch m4-ai-runs

Each model was asked for 33 quantities that follow the energy from the incident photon to the absorbed dose, five times per condition, and for 1,000 sampled photon interactions. Answers are scored against the study's Monte Carlo reference (Geant4 option4, boundary-free dose; D/Φ₀ = 5.3672e-12 Gy cm²).

What the runs show

  • Python makes the dose right for two models. With a sandbox, Claude Haiku 5.5 and DeepSeek V4 Flash put D/Φ₀, kerma, β, sw,air and PDD(5) within the 2% tolerance in both tool conditions.
  • Without tools, every model misses the dose. The closest A0 answer is Claude Haiku 5.5 at -2.6%; the others are off by 5% to 18%, and Gemini by a factor of about 3,000.
  • Tools do not help the other three. GPT-6 Luna stays 7–11% low on D/Φ₀ and 20% high on the mean photon energy. Qwen3.7 Flash moves further from the reference with tools. Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite made no Python call on the observables task, answered with zeros in two A1 runs and reported dose in the wrong unit in A2.
  • Every model breaks at Layer 1. The breakpoint is the first layer with a resolved failure. Spectral and angular shapes must match the Monte Carlo within three times its own half-sample distance, and no answer does, even where the scalar quantities pass.
  • Sampled events. Haiku and DeepSeek events are almost all physically valid under the bound-electron rule; Gemini and Qwen produce 28–66% invalid events. GPT-6 Luna declined 9 of its 10 event calls, saying it cannot sample without a transport code.

Read these results with

  • The models are each provider's fast, low-cost tier, not the most capable model (D030). Qwen3.7 Flash fills an open-weight slot but has no published weights.
  • Haiku runs with prompt caching to stay inside the $10 budget; the model sees the same prompts (D032).
  • Event answers count with 150 to 250 events; the prompt asks for 200 (D031).
  • Gemini was rate-limited by its upstream throughout; its four API-error conversations were rerun (D033, D035).
  • A malformed fenced JSON block gets a misleading retry message in this harness version (D034). It affects which answers are readable, not how readable answers score.
  • The Monte Carlo reference is final (M2, not provisional). Raw conversations are in ai/raw/M4-R0/; scores in analysis/results/M5_M4-R0.json.
