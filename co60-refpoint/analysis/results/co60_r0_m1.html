Findings

What to look at

The s_w,air basis matters more than the statistics

The same Monte Carlo electron spectrum gives sw,air = 1.1331 with ICRU 37 stopping powers, 1.1268 with ICRU 90 and 1.1385 with Geant4's own. That is a 1% spread, against a statistical uncertainty below 10−5. Geant4 option4 uses the Penelope ionisation model below 100 keV; for water its collision stopping power is 2.5% above Bethe–Møller (I = 78 eV) at 12 keV and 1.7% at 100 keV, smoothed into the higher-energy model above that. Air agrees within 0.1%. Which basis the AI answers are scored against is a gate decision.

Geant4's general gamma process drops Rayleigh scattering above 1.022 MeV

In option4 (Geant4 11.4.3) G4GammaGeneralProcess does not sample coherent scattering in its 2mec²–100 MeV zone, which contains both Co-60 lines. It showed up as zero Rayleigh events among primary photons in the first production attempt. That run was archived and all production runs switch the general process off (DECISIONS D019).

The plan's linear electron-fluence estimator fails closure

Spreading each step's track length linearly over its energy loss puts too much fluence at low energy when Geant4 ends an electron in one step: the dose rebuilt from that fluence is 6.4% too high. Spreading it as 1/L(E) closes to 0.06%. Layer 4 uses the CSDA-weighted estimator; the linear one is reported alongside (DECISIONS D020). On sw,air the two differ by 0.008%.

The scoring cylinder's boundary distorts the dose next to it

The depth scan shows the dose 2–3.7% high just in front of the scoring cylinder and 1.5–2.3% low just behind it, in water on both sides. Moving the cylinder to 8 cm moves the pattern. Far from both cylinders the two depth scans agree to 0.1%. Inside the cylinder, the dose agrees with the boundary-free dose at the same depth (row in the checks table, from a run with the main run's variance reduction and the cylinder moved to 8 cm). The likely cause is the electron step limit near boundaries (GS multiple scattering, SafetyPlus), which moves energy deposition without creating any. How to score from M2 on is a gate question.

A primary-fluence fluctuation that looked like a 4σ effect

The main run's primary fluence came out 0.12% below the analytic value, and its batch spread made that look like 4σ. Eight diagnostic runs and a transmission measurement of μ found no cause. The main run's batches agreed with each other better than their history variance allows, so its error bar was too small (DECISIONS D023). A replicate with new seeds, pooled by a rule fixed before it finished (D022), came out normal; the pooled value is in the checks table.

Ten-batch error bars can be too small

The standard error from 10 batches is itself uncertain by about ±24%, and in the main run it came out 0.4–0.7 of the history-by-history value. Reported uncertainties are now the larger of the two where both exist (DECISIONS D023), and the statistical targets are checked against that larger error.

Gate

Decisions for the M1 gate

  1. s_w,air reference basis for M4 scoring. ICRU 37 (1.1331, protocol-consistent), ICRU 90 (1.1268, current data), or score against both.
  2. Uncertainty policy (D023). Report the larger of the batch and history-by-history standard errors.
  3. Layer 4 estimator (D020). CSDA-weighted spectrum as the reference, linear estimator reported alongside.
  4. Fano +0.16 ± 0.06%. Accept as a bound on interface artifacts now, or wait for the step-size diagnostics, which run after production.
  5. Scoring-volume boundary. D at R0 is unaffected within the bound in the checks table, but the dose next to the cylinder is distorted by up to ±3%. Keep the mass-geometry scoring cylinder for M2 onward, or score in a parallel world or by position?
  6. refs.yaml. All external values, including TRS-398's 1.133 and the constants used for normalisation, are still unverified.
